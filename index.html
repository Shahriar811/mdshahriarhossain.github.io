02 / THE PERSON

Code. Curiosity.
A bit of craft.

✳

Hi, I’m Shahriar. I like making complex things feel simple.

I’m a fourth-year Computer Science & Engineering student at North South University in Dhaka. My focus is native Android: responsive interfaces, clean architecture, and the small details that make an app feel right.

I work with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, using MVVM, Hilt, and local databases to build maintainable apps. I’m especially interested in on-device AI—running language models locally with MediaPipe so prompts can stay on the phone.

Away from the editor, you’ll find me lifting weights or looking for a good photograph. Different tools, same attention to detail.
