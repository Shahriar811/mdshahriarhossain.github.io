An IoT intrusion-detection system combining XGBoost predictions, local reinforcement learning, and Flower-based federated training. Three clients share model weights while raw network flows stay local.

Dataset & preprocessing

The notebook loads ASEADOS_SDN_IoT.csv: 457,044 raw flows become 454,112 after removing 2,932 duplicate rows. Identifier columns, constant features, and highly correlated features are removed, leaving 42 numeric inputs. Min-max scaling is fitted on the training split.

Training rebalancing produces 1,039,710 samples for selected experiments and federated shards. This is a resampled training set, not an additional raw dataset.

Models used & recorded performance

12 traditional models and 8 deep-learning models are recorded in the supervised results. The RL experiments include DQN-Basic, DQN-Improved, PPO, Rainbow-DQN, and QR-DQN. XGBoost is the dashboard’s main predictor; the current federated pipeline averages a Dueling DQN agent with Flower / FedAvg.

Explainability & deployment

The Flask interface compares supervised, local RL, and federated decisions, and reads server/client status files. The project’s saved SHAP chart shows which features influence XGBoost predictions.

Data behind these figures

Dataset counts come from the saved outputs in IDS_FL_RL_Combined.ipynb. Supervised scores come from results_summary.csv; RL scores come from model_info.json. Custom and federated metadata are read from their saved PyTorch checkpoints using weights-only loading. F1 is weighted unless marked macro. These are recorded experiment results.
