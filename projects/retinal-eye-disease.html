Explainable AI-Based Eye Disease Classification Using Retinal Image

University research into retinal-image classification with pretrained vision models and a lightweight custom CNN. Grad-CAM++ explains model attention, while FedAvg and FedProx support federated experiments. Includes web, API, and Android interfaces.

Dataset & sample distribution

The notebook indexes 16,242 images across 10 classes from the Eye Disease Image Dataset’s augmented directory. A stratified 70 / 15 / 15 split produces 11,369 training, 2,436 validation, and 2,437 test images.

Vision models & performance

The backbone experiment compares DenseNet121, ResNet50, EfficientNet-B0, MobileNetV3-Small, and ViT Small. A separate lightweight OurCNN experiment uses 122,972 parameters, compared with 7,978,856 for the DenseNet121 reference—about 64.9× fewer parameters.

Explainable & federated learning

Grad-CAM++ visualizes model attention. Federated experiments use MobileNetV3-Small across three clients with FedAvg and FedProx. These recorded round scores use the validation set.

Deployment connects a Streamlit interface, FastAPI backend, and Android client.
